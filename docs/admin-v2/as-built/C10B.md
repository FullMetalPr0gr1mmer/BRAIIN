### Decisions (crm.md §10's two open points)

1. **A Sales confirm spends a slot of the reveal's own limiter**, not a limit of its own. It
   brings another lead's e-mail and phone to the contact the next reveal decrypts, which is
   what revealing that lead would have cost; so what a confirm brings has cost the Sales user a
   slot. Every door that adds a channel keeps to C6's ceiling (since the review, the arrival's
   link too), so one reveal shows at most ten e-mails and ten phones of one contact: values a
   person added or copied, and values that arrived with the person's own inquiries, which cost
   no slot and which the ceiling, not the limiter, bounds.
2. **The reveal shows a Sales user every channel**, not only the primary of each kind: the
   channel editor (make primary, add) works on the revealed values, and the ceiling bounds
   them. Removing one is Admin's (crm.erase) since the review. Asked of the owner below.
   (Superseded 2026-11-13 by the owner's decisions: a Sales reveal shows the main e-mail and
   phone only, and choosing the main one is an Admin's; C-LAST-INTEGRATION.md section 10.)
3. **(Review) A standing rule for later slices:** any door that brings another record's
   e-mails or phones into a contact (C12's merge first, then any other) claims a `pii-reveal`
   slot for the actor when `!receivesLeadPiiUnasked(role)`, after the live recheck and before
   anything is read, and keeps to the ceiling. Recorded in crm.md §10 and CLAUDE.md's C10b
   bullet.
