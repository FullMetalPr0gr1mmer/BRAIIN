## Plan (written before building; kept current)

Status: planned (2026-11-11).

### Scope

Sales gains `crm.contacts` and `crm.tasks`, and nothing else: no `crm.erase` (an Admin erases),
no `crm.settings`, no `export.csv` or `export.backup`. Developer still holds no `crm.*`
(owner default O-7). The invariants hold for Sales (`crm.contacts ⇒ leads.manage ∧ leads.pii`,
`crm.tasks ⇒ crm.contacts`).

### SQL (0136)

1. `app.role_crm_contacts` and `app.role_crm_tasks` re-created with `('admin', 'sales')`, in
   the form every `app.role_*` helper has (immutable, inlined), grants restated. Every policy,
   live check, door and owner rule C5, C6 and C8 wrote calls these helpers, so nothing else
   changes in SQL: the permissive policies, the RESTRICTIVE live checks (`app.live_role()`),
   `app.crm_contacts_actor_ok`, the contact and task owner rules, the task insert guard and
   `crm_nav_counts` all follow.
2. `public.crm_retention_months()`: an INVOKER door over C6's `app.crm_retention_months()`,
   answering only for a live `crm.contacts` holder. The contact page read
   `site_settings.retention` as the caller, which only CMS staff read (`app.is_staff()`), so
   Sales would have lost the "kept until" line.
3. The help guides: `the-sales-role` (revision 1) and `roles-and-access` (the revision the
   migrations before it leave) say that Sales works contacts and tasks too, with the reveal
   one person at a time and no erasure.
4. Postconditions: both helpers answer Admin and Sales only; `crm.erase`, `crm.settings`
   and the other helpers unchanged for Sales; the door's kind, pin and grants.

### Worker

- `ROLE_CAPS.sales`: `crm.contacts` and `crm.tasks` full. Nav is capability-driven, so Sales'
  sidebar gains Contacts and Tasks (with its overdue count), and the palette's Contacts group.
- **One reveal stays one person** (crm.md §10, C6-SEC-3): a phone-match confirm by a role that
  cannot export (`receivesLeadPiiUnasked` false, so Sales) spends a slot of the shared
  `pii-reveal` limiter (fail-closed, after the live recheck, before anything is read), because
  it copies another lead's e-mail and phone into the contact the next reveal decrypts. With
  C6's ten-per-kind ceiling, a Sales reveal shows at most twenty values of one person, and
  every value it shows cost a slot. The reveal shows every channel (not only the primary of
  each kind): the channel editor needs them, and the ceiling bounds them.
- The contact page reads the retention horizon through the new door.
- Users & roles: Sales' one line names contacts and tasks.
- Sales' default quick actions gain "My tasks".
- Comments that said "Sales from C10b" now say it holds them.
