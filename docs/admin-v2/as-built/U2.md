## What was built

- **Three live defects ended.** `GET /api/admin/site-health` scanned up to 20,000 raw
  `web_vitals` rows in the Worker (Pillar 4: dashboards read `rollup_*` only; PostgREST
  returned 1,000 of them anyway). The stats routes asked for 5,000 rollup or search rows and
  got 1,000, so a long range came back short. And `app.rollup_pageviews` (0010) recomputed a
  trailing 72 hours, not whole days, with a SET upsert: the earliest day in the window was set
  to the count of its rows after the run's time of day, so every day's final total was about
  its last quarter hour (found here, beyond the brief; below).
- **The vitals rollup.** `public.rollup_daily_vitals (tenant_id, day, path, metric, p75,
  samples, hist, updated_at)`, one row per tenant, UTC day, path and metric, `'*'` for the whole
  site. `p75` is `percentile_disc(0.75)` (exact for the day); `hist` counts the samples in 25
  fixed buckets whose 24 upper edges are `app.vitals_edges(metric)`, the §6 budgets and web.dev's
  "poor" thresholds among them. A bucket holds the values above its lower edge up to and
  including its upper edge, so "within budget" (at most the budget, web.dev's "good") is exact
  for any range, though a range's p75 is shown as its bucket's upper edge. RLS ENABLE + FORCE,
  one policy (`rollup_vitals_read`: the tenant predicate, then `app.role_site_health`), staff
  SELECT, the service role the four writes, nothing for anon or PUBLIC, postconditions under
  both grant regimes (MAINTAIN on 17).
- **`app.rollup_vitals(p_days)`**, hourly by pg_cron (`rollup-vitals`, minute 7; the Worker's
  cron is near its 50 subrequests): the last `p_days` whole UTC days and today, every tenant, a
  SET (a late beacon corrects its day), a path cut at `?` and `#` then at 200 characters (one
  that does not start with `/` afterwards, a client's `*` included, counts in `'*'` only, so it
  can never collide with the site row), the 100 busiest paths per tenant, day and metric (the
  rest in `'*'` only), rows the run no longer produces in its window removed, rows older than
  400 days purged (D9). Backfilled for 90 days in the migration.
- **The readers**, SECURITY INVOKER with an empty search_path, each fenced to the token's
  tenant (crm_people's rule), bounds a 22023: `vitals_summary(p_tenant, p_days 1..90)`
  (per path and metric; one day's exact p75, more days' from the summed histograms, `approx`,
  `open`; `'*'` and the 100 paths with most samples), `pageview_summary(p_tenant, p_from,
  p_to, p_locale)` (total, by day EN and AR, by language, top 20, `first_day`; at most 400 days),
  `pageview_paths(..., p_limit 1..100, p_offset)` (a page, with the number of paths),
  `search_summary(p_tenant, p_days, p_locale)` (total, how many found nothing, top 50, the 50
  that found nothing most), and `content_health(p_tenant)` (the checklist's counts, below).
- **Helpers:** `app.role_site_health` (`siteHealth.view`) and `app.role_analytics_read`
  (`analytics.read`), immutable, executed by staff and the service role as 0033's;
  `rollup_read` restated on the second (the same four roles), so Sales gains stats through one
  line (D6). Both in `tests/authz/sqlHelpers.spec.ts`.
- **`/admin/analytics`**, server-rendered, zero JS: range links (7, 30, 90; `?range=`), in-page

## Departures from the brief, and why

- **`rollup_daily_vitals` departs from CLAUDE.md §8's row shape** (every table: `id`,
  `tenant_id`, timestamps, `created_by`/`updated_by`). It is a derived rollup, like
  `rollup_daily_pageviews` (0002): keyed by what it aggregates, `(tenant_id, day, path,
  metric)`, with no `id`, no `created_at` and no actor columns, because no person writes a row
  (the rollup does, as its owner) and a row is recomputed, not edited. `updated_at` says when
  the rollup last changed it (`vitals_summary`'s `rolled_up_at`). `tenant_id` and RLS ENABLE +
  FORCE, the tenant predicate first, are kept as §8 requires.
- **`app.rollup_pageviews` restated (beyond the brief).** The 0010 window cut the earliest day
  at the run's time of day (above), so the stats would have shown wrong totals however they
  were read. Whole UTC days now (the window starts at midnight p_days days ago, so it covers
  everything the old one did), unchanged rows not rewritten, the same signature, privileges and
  job; re-rolled once for the 90-day raw horizon (the partition purge keeps whole months past
  90 days, so those days are complete). Days older than 90 keep their undercounted totals: the
  raw rows are gone. Owner item below.
- **`app.vitals_edges` is executable by `authenticated`** (the brief: by no API role).
  `vitals_summary` is an invoker so that RLS decides what it reads, and it reads the edges as
  its caller; the edges are constants published in the migration. The rollup and the table's
  CHECK do not need it (the CHECK is the constant 25, so a writer needs no EXECUTE).
- **A fifth reader, `content_health(p_tenant)`.** The checklist's content and SEO rows need the
  published entities against `entity_seo` and the attention view's counts; one invoker RPC
  instead of five Worker reads (and no capped read of the attention view). Every row it reads
  is one any staff role may read.
- **`pageview_summary` takes `p_locale`** and **`search_summary` returns `zero_total`**, so the
  JSON twin's `?locale=` and the search KPI are exact rather than recomputed from capped lists.
- **New leads is `total`, not `new`.** `lead_summary`'s `new` is the leads still in the first
  stage; a figure for a period must not shrink as the team works through it, so the KPI counts
  every non-spam lead that arrived in the range.
- **The refusal is `.msg[data-kind='error']`, not the kit Note.** The islands' refusal markup
  (`describeError`), so both kinds of locked screen look and are found the same.
- **ServerTable's scroll box is a focusable named region** (`tabindex="0"`, `role="region"`,
  the caption as its name), for every server table: a table wider than a phone with no link in
  it left its scrolling out of keyboard reach (axe `scrollable-region-focusable`). The Speed and
  Errors tables are the kit's markup with the same region (their cells hold badges and
  `<details>`, which ServerTable's typed cells cannot).
- **Within budget means at most the budget** (web.dev's "good"); §6 writes "<". The two differ
  only at the boundary sample; the edges make the bucket rule exact either way, and web.dev's
  reading is the one the field data is defined by.
- **Not ported:** the prototype's `.sc` score chips, gauge and uptime strip (no Lighthouse or
  uptime figures are shown; owner item O-20 for uptime).
- **The Default description row** checks the site default in both languages (fixable on SEO
  defaults); pages relying on their own summary are information, not a failure: there is no
  per-page SEO editor yet, so a failing row nobody could fix would drag the score for ever.
- **The SEO tab's "Built in" rows** (sitemap, hreflang, robots, structured data in CI) are code
  facts, held to the files by the checklist spec, outside the score.
