## Departures from the design, and why

- **Columns.** `leads` gains `stage_id` (composite key), `is_spam`, `lead_number` (per tenant,
  from a counter, never reused), `first_response_at`, `won_at`, `version` and actor columns.
  CRM-1's other columns arrive later: the indexes, score, source and channel with C1b (0035),
  `search_text` with C2a (0036), and assignee, value, star, read, tags, last contact and the
  attribution columns with the slices that use them.
- **Stages.** `lead_stages` per tenant, seeded New / Contacted / Proposal sent / Won / Lost: a
  fixed tone palette, one initial stage, 2 to 10 stages with a won and a lost, checked at
  commit; new tenants are seeded by a trigger. Staff read them; editing arrives with CRM
  settings.
- **One trigger function per phase.** The design listed separate triggers (sync, spam, version
  bump, actor, stamps). As built, `app.tg_lead_pipeline` is the one BEFORE phase, in a written
  order: the number, the legacy status and stage sync (legacy done becomes Lost, O-8), the spam
  horizon, the stamps, the version (it moves only with the stage or the spam flag), and
  `updated_at` and `updated_by` (0002's timestamps trigger on `leads` is dropped). A write that
  changes only what the CRM derives (indexes, score, the band moving into the encrypted
  timeline) keeps `updated_at` and `updated_by`. `app.tg_lead_timeline` is the AFTER phase.
- **The legacy notes mirror** is one note per lead (a unique partial index on source
  `legacy`), rewritten in place on each save and removed when the field is cleared, so text
  taken out of the field leaves the thread too. Thread notes stay append-only (the service role
  holds no UPDATE). The mirror runs one way until C4 and C14.
- **A note's `note_added` event** is written by an AFTER INSERT trigger on `lead_notes`, in the
  statement that adds the note, so a failure leaves neither and a retry cannot duplicate.
- **Spam (D2).** Marking spam, a legacy `status = 'spam'` write included, caps retention at
  `spam_days` and saves the old horizon; clearing gives it back, but never past a horizon set
  while flagged or by the same write, so a documented erasure stands. Existing spam leads were
  capped by the migration.
- **Stamps.** `first_response_at` is stamped only when a lead leaves the initial stage, and
  `won_at` only when it enters a won stage; never on an unrelated write, and not for leads the
  backfill mapped past New.
- **The backfill** is `app.backfill_lead_pipeline()`, keyed on `stage_id is null` and numbering
  after the counter, so it is re-runnable and pgTAP runs it on legacy-shaped rows.
- **Grants under both regimes (P-14).** Supabase's bootstrap grants `service_role` ALL on a new
  table, which would have failed the append-only postcondition, so `lead_notes` is revoked from
  `public`, `anon`, `authenticated` and `service_role`, then granted SELECT, INSERT and DELETE
  to `service_role`.
- **Backups.** The CRM tables are in `FORBIDDEN_BACKUP_TABLES`, and `exportBackup.spec` fails
  on any `lead_` or `crm_` table a migration creates without forbidding it.
- **Tests.** pgTAP `rls_lead_pipeline.test.sql` and `lead_pipeline_backfill.test.sql`, and a
  check that the service role's plain lead insert (the public form's path) passes every new
  trigger.
