## Plan (written before building, kept current)

1. **Capability `crm.contacts`, Admin only** (Developer none, owner default O-7; Sales only from
   C10b). `ROLE_CAPS`, CLAUDE.md §5, architecture §3.4 and §9.3, `capLabels`, `roleMatrix`,
   `matrix.spec` (36 capabilities, the invariants `crm.contacts ⇒ leads.manage ∧ leads.pii`
   and `crm.erase ⇒ crm.contacts`), `sqlHelpers.spec` (`app.role_crm_contacts`).
2. **Tables** (RLS ENABLE + FORCE, P-14 grants under both regimes, the tenant predicate first in
   every policy, a RESTRICTIVE live check on each):
   - `crm_contacts`: display name, generated `name_key` and `search_text`, title, company name,
     stage, source, owner (a composite key to a profile, `ON DELETE SET NULL (owner_id)`, an
     active `crm.contacts` holder), locale, city, country, tags (`app.tags_ok`), custom (an
     object under 8 KB), star, `last_activity_at`, `merged_into`/`merged_at` (C12),
     version and actor columns. Staff (`crm.contacts`): SELECT, and UPDATE of the detail
     columns; no INSERT or DELETE (adding a contact needs encryption, so it is C6's
     service-role door; removal is erasure or retention).
   - `crm_contact_channels`: kind e-mail or phone, the lead's ciphertext copied as is
     (`value_enc`), `value_hmac`, primary flag, source. One e-mail per tenant (partial unique
     index); phones are not unique. Staff read the safe columns only, never the value or the
     index.
   - `crm_contact_notes`: as `lead_notes` is built (0034): no policy, the service role only
     (the audited door is C6's), append-only.
   - `crm_contact_events`: the contact's timeline, metadata only, written by definer
     triggers and the service-role doors; staff read under the live check.
3. **Leads**: `contact_id` (composite key, `ON DELETE SET NULL (contact_id)`) and
   `contact_link` (how it was linked: `email`, `phone`, `new`, or `staff` from C6; null =
   never). `phone` IS the flag ("Linked by phone, please check"). Both readable by lead
   workers and appended to `leads_safe`; no staff UPDATE grant yet (C6). Both are derived for
   the BEFORE phase (not an edit, no version move): `app.tg_lead_pipeline` restated with them
   in `c_derived` and nothing else changed.
4. **Linking** (`app.crm_link_lead_core`, a definer, the service role's doors only):
   e-mail blind index first (a per-address advisory lock plus the unique index, so two leads
   from one new address make one contact); else a phone match links to the most recently
   active contact with that phone and flags it (its e-mail is NOT added to that contact
   until a person checks); else a new contact with the lead's channels. Spam leads are never
   linked. Writes `contact_linked` on the lead's timeline and `lead_linked` on the contact's.
   A linked lead in a won stage makes its contact a client; an assignee who holds
   `crm.contacts` fills an empty owner (an AFTER trigger on leads, inert for unlinked leads).
5. **Ingest** (`crm_ingest_lead` restated, same allow-list): after the insert, the link runs in
   its own sub-transaction; any error there is swallowed (a WARNING), the lead stays, and the
   cron links it later. A public lead is never blocked or lost by the link.
6. **Backfill**: `public.crm_link_backfill(p_limit)` (service role), run by the daily cron's
   CRM step right after the index backfill (`src/lib/crm/linkBackfill.ts`, one subrequest, no
   decryption). Only leads never linked (`contact_link` null), indexed, not spam.
7. **Activity and retention**: `last_activity_at` advances on a lead linked, a lead created,
   moved, contacted, noted; a contact note; a contact stage change (not on detail edits,
   stars, assignment, value or spam). `app.purge_crm()` from `app.run_retention()` (restated):
   contacts idle past `leads_months`, and orphan auto-contacts (made by the system, never
   edited, not starred, no lead, no note).
8. **Erasure**: `public.crm_erase_preview` and `public.crm_erase_person` (service role,
   `app.crm_eraser_ok` live). The person's leads (linked, or sharing one of its e-mail
   indexes), the phone-only matches the Admin ticked (each must be one), then the contact
   (channels, notes, events by cascade). `crm_erase_lead` restated to remove an orphan
   auto-contact with its lead.
9. **Docs**: CLAUDE.md §3, §5, §8, §9; architecture §3.4/§9.3; retention.md; migrations.md;
   README; the privacy-notice text (EN/AR) and the register.
10. **Tests**: pgTAP `rls_crm_contacts`, `crm_contacts_link`, `crm_contacts_retention_erase`
    over admin, content_creator, seo, developer, sales, anon, other_tenant and stale tokens;
    `rls_sales_role` fixtures; vitest for the link backfill, the cron budget, the matrix,
    the helpers and the backup list.
