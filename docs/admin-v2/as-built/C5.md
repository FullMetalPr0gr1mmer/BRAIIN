## Departures from the design, and why

- **The phone links and flags** (README's C5 row, which wins over crm.md §5.2's "never
  auto-linked"). A phone match is linked with `contact_link = 'phone'` and adds nothing to that
  contact: the lead's e-mail is not attached until a person checks (C6/C7), so a wrong match
  never makes later e-mails from that address auto-join the wrong person. A lead whose e-mail
  matches no contact but whose phone does is linked by phone (flagged), not given a new
  contact; the merge finder (C12) and the check (C6) resolve it.
- **No staff INSERT or DELETE on contacts, and no staff write of the lead link.** The design
  had INVOKER `crm_create_contact`/`crm_update_contact` and a `contact_id` UPDATE grant here.
  Adding a contact carries an e-mail or phone, which only the Worker can encrypt and index, so
  it is C6's service-role door; a direct UPDATE grant on `leads.contact_id` would be held by
  every lead worker (Developer included, who has no `crm.contacts`: a column grant cannot tell
  roles apart). C6 adds the link writes with the API that checks `crm.contacts`.
- **`crm_contact_notes` has no policy** (service role only), as `lead_notes` is built (0034,
  C2b), not the design's "like lead_notes" with a read policy: notes are read through an audited
  door, so "one at a time" holds at PostgREST too.
- **The link is not an edit and moves no version** (crm.md §4.3 listed `contact_id` among the
  versioned fields): the backfill reaches old leads, and a version move would 409 an open
  screen and make every old lead look edited tonight. It is derived, as the indexes are.
- **Activity is narrower than "a lead event"** (crm.md §5.1): an assignment, a value, spam, a
  deleted note and the link itself are not the person's activity, and counting them would keep
  a person's details longer for no reason (data minimisation). A new contact's activity starts
  at its lead's arrival, not at the link, so the backfill does not give old leads' people a
  fresh 24 months.
- **No `domain_hmac` or `is_freemail` on channels**: the lead carries no domain index to copy;
  they arrive with companies (C11), which needs them.
- **The won rule and the owner fill are ordinary contact edits**: put down to the session
  that moved or assigned the lead (or nobody), they move the contact's version like any change,
  so such a contact is no longer an orphan candidate.
- **`crm_erase_lead` takes an orphan auto-contact** (the design left that to the next nightly
  run): its channels are a copy of the erased lead's details.
- **A tombstone's `merged_into` is `ON DELETE CASCADE`**, not SET NULL: SET NULL would break the
  pair check (`merged_at` without `merged_into`) at the survivor's purge or erasure, and a
  tombstone of a person who is gone is that person's.

## Residual risks and owner questions

- **Unverified e-mail** (accepted by design): an inquiry that names someone else's address
  joins that person's contact (its message under their record, their activity moved). The
  arriving lead never changes the contact's details, and the lead itself is as before.
- **The backfill's queue**: a lead whose link fails every night stays at the head of the queue
  (oldest first); 200 such leads would stall it. Each failure is counted on the indexing line.
- **Concurrency is proven by construction, not by test**: pgTAP has one session, so "two
  arrivals from one new address make one contact" is shown sequentially (the second joins the
  first); the advisory lock and the unique index's conflict branch cover the concurrent case.
- **Visual baselines**: `/admin/users` (the roles matrix gains a row) and the account drawer's
  "What you can do" (Admin gains a line) differ from the committed admin baselines; re-make them
  with the hand-run "Admin visual baselines" workflow after the merge.
- **O-11 / L-2** (HIGH, open): the notice text, the lawful basis, retention from last activity,
  phone linking, the backup period, the 30-day answer, attribution (C13), the UK hosting.
- **Backups**: the notice states 28 days; production today has Supabase's own backups only
  (launch runbook §9). The number must be checked against production before the words go live.
