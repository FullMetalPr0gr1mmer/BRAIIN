## Plan (written before building; kept current)

Status: planned (2026-11-11).

### Constraints that shaped it

- **C5's "a person edited it" rule.** `app.tg_crm_contact_row` marks a contact `staff_edited`
  on any *direct* write that changes a detail (`pg_trigger_depth() = 1`), and an auto-contact
  that is `staff_edited` never goes with its last inquiry (the orphan rule, C5-SEC-1). So the
  system's company link must write the contact from inside a trigger, as C5's own won rule and
  owner fill do; a service-role door that updated the contact directly would keep every
  auto-linked contact alive after its inquiries' erasure.
- **No restatement of another slice's function.** `crm_ingest_lead` (C5, and C13 in parallel),
  `app.tg_lead_pipeline`, `app.purge_crm`, `app.run_retention` and the erasure doors are
  restated by C5, C8, C12 and C13; a fourth copy would make the merge train pick one silently.
  Everything here is new functions and new triggers.

### SQL (0137)

1. **`public.crm_companies`**: id; tenant_id; name (1 to 120, no control characters); `name_key`
   (generated, `app.crm_name_key`, C5's folding, trigram index); sector_id and client_id
   (composite keys to the company's own tenant's `sectors` and `clients`, ON DELETE SET NULL of
   that column; `clients.visible` stays the disclosure switch, owned by `portfolio.write`: a link
   publishes nothing); size (`1-10`, `11-50`, `51-200`, `200+`); website (https, at most 2048);
   city (at most 80); stage (`lead`, `client`, `past`, `partner`, as contacts); owner_id
   (composite key to profiles, SET NULL, trigger-checked: an active `crm.contacts` holder of the
   tenant); notes (at most 2000); `domain_hmac` (the domain blind index, 64 hex, one company per
   domain per tenant; never readable or writable by staff); `staff_edited` (as contacts); version
   and actor columns; unique (tenant_id, id). `sectors` and `clients` gain `unique (tenant_id, id)`
   for the composite keys (0041's precedent for profiles).
2. **RLS**: ENABLE + FORCE; permissive per command through `app.role_crm_contacts` (tenant
   predicate first); RESTRICTIVE `crm_companies_live` for ALL (`app.live_role()`, H16). Grants
   stated (P-14): authenticated SELECT on every column but `domain_hmac`, INSERT and UPDATE on
   the person-chosen columns, DELETE; the service role all; anon and PUBLIC nothing.
3. **`app.tg_crm_company_row`** (BEFORE, definer): C8-SEC-2's insert guard first (an API
   session's insert names the token's tenant and holds a live `crm.contacts` role, else 42501
   with RLS's words, before any rule reads), the owner rule, the stamps, the version (an edit of
   a chosen column), `staff_edited` on a direct write that changes one.
4. **`crm_contacts.company_id`**: composite key to a company of the contact's own tenant,
   ON DELETE SET NULL (company_id); index; staff UPDATE column grant. The free-text
   `company_name` stays (C7's field keeps working until a contact is linked): a BEFORE trigger
   `crm_contacts_company` (named to fire before C5's `crm_contacts_row`) makes `company_name`
   the linked company's name whenever `company_id` is set, and a company rename re-writes its
   contacts' `company_name` from an AFTER trigger (a system write: no `staff_edited`). Every
   C6 reader, the list, search, the palette and the Tasks screen show the linked company with no
   restatement. Unlinking leaves the last name as free text.
5. **Find-or-create at arrival** (README's C11 row), never blocking the public form:
   - **by name**: `app.tg_lead_company` (AFTER UPDATE OF contact_id ON leads, after C5's
     `leads_contact`): when a lead is linked other than by phone and is not spam, its contact
     has no company and no person has edited it, the company the lead names (C5's `name_key`
     folding) is found, oldest first, under a per-name advisory lock, or created (`created_by`
     null: the system's), and the contact linked from inside the trigger. The whole step is
     inside its own `begin … exception` block: an error is a WARNING and the link stands.
   - **by domain**: the Worker works out the e-mail's domain, and for a non-freemail one its
     blind index (crm.md §6.1, kind `domain`), and after the lead is in calls
     `public.crm_lead_domain(p_tenant, p_lead, p_domain_hmac)` (service role only): it records
     the index on the lead's e-mail channel (`crm_contact_channels.domain_hmac`, a new column
     staff never read), and that channel's AFTER trigger links a contact with no company to the
     company holding the domain, or teaches the contact's company its domain when it has none
     and no other company holds it. The call is wrapped: any failure is a `system_logs`
     warning (the code only) and the inquiry's answer is unchanged.
6. **An auto-company goes with its last contact**: AFTER DELETE and AFTER UPDATE OF company_id
   on `crm_contacts` (definer): a company the system made (`created_by` null), never edited by a
   person and no longer named by any contact is deleted. So retention, a lead's erasure (its
   orphan auto-contact) and a person's erasure take the company an inquiry's free text made,
   with no restatement of `app.purge_crm` or the erasure doors.
7. **Readers and writers** (SECURITY INVOKER, authenticated only, the tenant named and checked):
   `crm_companies_list(p_tenant, p_filter)` (an allow-listed filter: q, stage, owner, sort,
   limit, offset; contacts count and won value derived, never stored: value = the won leads'
   `value_sar` over the companies' contacts, leads linked other than by phone),
   `crm_company_read(p_tenant, p_company)` (the company and its first 50 contacts, names and
   titles), `crm_company_create`, `crm_company_update` (versioned, 40001), `crm_company_delete`
   (versioned), `crm_contact_company(p_tenant, p_contact, p_version, p_action, p_company)`
   (`link`, `unlink`, `create`: "Create company from this name" finds the company by the
   contact's free-text name first, or makes it, and links it; one transaction).

### Migration path for the free-text company (recorded as asked)

`crm_contacts.company_name` stays the free text C7 edits. A contact linked to a company shows
the company's name in it (kept in step by the database), so every reader keeps working; an
unlinked contact keeps what was typed. A later contract slice may (1) run "Create company from
this name" for every unlinked contact with a free-text company, as a reviewed backfill, then
(2) make the field read-only for a linked contact in the API too, and finally (3) drop the
free text from contacts once every reader reads the company. Leads keep their own free-text
`company` (crm.md §5.3).
