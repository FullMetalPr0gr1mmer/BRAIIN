## What was built

### The data (0099)

- `site_profile` v2: `tagline` (bilingual, both halves or none, 1 to 120), `phone_e164` and
  `phone_display` (the display needs the number, the WhatsApp pattern), `map_url` (https, no
  credentials; the host allow-list, Google Maps and Apple Maps, is Zod's as the socials' is),
  `favicon_media_id` and `app_icon_media_id` (composite keys to `media_assets (tenant_id, id)`,
  U10's `media_assets_tenant_id_id`, ON DELETE RESTRICT, MATCH SIMPLE, each indexed where set),
  and the four footer settings E6 edits: `footer_copyright` (bilingual; no brace but `{year}`,
  `{brand}`, `{place}`), `footer_style` (light or dark), `footer_show_email`,
  `footer_show_social`, defaulting to today's footer. Every CHECK is `coalesce(…, false)`, so
  a missing half is refused, not passed as NULL.
- `app.tg_site_profile_icons`, an INVOKER trigger: a newly set icon must be a `storage` image,
  square, at least 512 px, read under the writer's RLS (and under `apply_release` as the
  publisher). A visible asset of the wrong shape is 23514 naming `site_profile_favicon_square`
  or `site_profile_app_icon_square`; one the writer cannot see is left to the foreign key
  (23503). It fires after the release guard and the applications guard, before the stamps.
- `site_settings.timezone`: the studio's one zone, immediate (P-3), a closed list equal to
  `crm_settings`' CHECK and to `STUDIO_TIME_ZONES` (`packages/schemas/timeZones.ts`; a spec
  parses both CHECKs). Every existing row took its tenant's `crm_settings.timezone` (only rows
  that differ are written, so no open form 409s); `crm_settings.timezone` is commented
  deprecated (C14 drops it).
- `app.role_general_settings(r)` (Admin, Developer), the helper for `settings.general`, held to
  `ROLE_CAPS` by `tests/authz/sqlHelpers.spec.ts`, which also holds `settings.general` and
  `maintenance.manage` to one role set while they share `site_settings` (P-9).
- RESTRICTIVE live writes on both rows (`site_profile_live_insert/update`,
  `site_settings_live_insert/update`, H10's pattern): a token whose profile no longer matches
  it (demoted, deactivated, locked, moved) writes nothing through PostgREST, the maintenance
  switch included (its route writes the row before KV).
- Grants stated under both Supabase regimes: anon SELECT on `site_profile`; authenticated and
  service_role SELECT, INSERT, UPDATE on both; nothing else. This **withdraws** the bootstrap
  ALL: DELETE, TRUNCATE, REFERENCES and TRIGGER for authenticated and service_role (the FOR ALL
  `site_settings_write` policy had admitted an Admin or Developer DELETE). The gate (rule 6):
  `tests/lib/noSingletonDelete.spec.ts` fails any code in `src/`, `scripts/` or
  `supabase/functions/` that deletes either row (the query builder, SQL, or an RPC naming
  either table), with `noTruncate.spec.ts` for TRUNCATE; the postconditions,
  `grants_app_schema.test.sql` assertions 30 and 31 and the read-back's
  `forbidden_privileges` and `authenticated_deletes_settings` hold the grants; launch runbook
  §10 says why the rollback line does not move and how to grant DELETE back (review finding
  u9-correctness-5).
- `app.studio_time_zone(p_tenant)`: DEFINER, tenant-fenced (another tenant's id answers the
  default), for C8 and C9a SQL (D4b: shipped); and `public.studio_time_zone()`, the Worker's
  door to it (INVOKER, the caller's own tenant, authenticated only), because Sales (C10a,
  0097) reads no `site_settings` row: a table read answered Sales Riyadh while every colleague
  saw the studio's zone (review finding u9-correctness-4).
- `media_assets_public_read`: 0073's text word for word, plus the icons (read under
  `site_profile`'s own RLS). No policy on `site_profile` may read `media_assets` (the recursion
  rule; a postcondition holds it). `public.media_usage` v6: v5 word for word plus
  `site_profile` rows labelled "favicon" and "app icon", `pending` false.
- The release registry's `site_profile` row restated (`do update`) with the ten columns, so a
  release stages them; `timezone` is never staged; `accepting_applications` stays exempt.
- Four guides through H11's catalog (below).

### The admin (U9a)

- `/admin/settings` (`AdminGeneral`, `screens/general/`): two `useEntitySave` editors on the
  field kit in the prototype's cards (EditorForm gains `groups` and `lockedFields`, additive;

  stays a 409 on the form's next save (review finding u9-correctness-3). The facts the
  screen cannot change (domain, languages, the two logos, which are build files) are the
  page's own zero-JS card.
- The singleton kernel gains `constraintFields` (a named CHECK or trigger refusal is a 422 on
  its field) and `afterWrite` (best effort after a live write; never on a staged save).
- The studio's clock: `relTime.ts` becomes `clockFor(tz)` (one formatter per zone);
  `studioTimeZone(locals)` reads the setting once per request through

### Maintenance v2 (U9b)

- One KV value, `site:maintenance` (`packages/schemas/maintenance.ts`): the switch, the
  allowlist (IPv4 as written, IPv6 canonicalised by the URL parser, at save and at compare),
  the bilingual headline and message, the background ("The hero image", the blurred still, or
  black), the contact buttons (a snapshot of `site_profile`), "Back on" (the day and its
  instant: 00:00 in the studio's zone), show the date, and the staff bypass. Read leniently
  per field: unreadable is maintenance off (fail open) with the bypass off (fail closed); a v1
  value reads as v2 with the bypass off.
- The middleware's block 1: in force while `active` and before "Back on" (the site returns at
  the instant with no write); the allowlist; then, only when the value allows it, the staff
  bypass, `src/lib/http/maintenanceBypass.ts`: a local pre-filter that never grants (a
  session cookie, a readable token, not expired, a member role), then the admin's own
  `resolveAuthContext` in a 1.5 s race. Only a whole context passes: no session, an error, a
  timeout, a session to revoke or one held at its second step (U17) is the 503. No side
  effects (never signs out, never clears a cookie), so it never renews a session either: an
  access token that expired while its owner was away is the 503 until any admin page renews
  it (renewing here would rotate the refresh token on a public response). The field's hint,
  the guide and launch runbook §6n say to open an admin page first, not to sign in again
  (review finding u9-correctness-13). A bypassed response is
  `private, no-store`, untagged and noindex. Exemptions unchanged: `/admin`, `/api`,
  `/healthz`, `/_image`.
- The 503 (`src/lib/maintenance/page.ts`): bilingual by the route (`lang="ar" dir="rtl"`),
  the copy or the built-in words, "Back on <day>" in our own month words with Arabic-Indic
  digits, "Email us" and "WhatsApp" (only with a number), every value escaped, one nonced
  `<style>`, fonts from `/fonts`, no script, `no-store`, `Retry-After` counting down to "Back
  on" (60 s to a day; 3600 without one), no `noindex`.
- The route: GET is the flat form plus what is in force (`effective`) and whether the edge
  agrees (`edgeActive`); PATCH re-checks the profile live, refuses a "Back on" that is not
  tomorrow to 30 days ahead (422 on `until`), snapshots the contact, writes the row
  (version-checked, behind the live policy) then KV (`kvSynced` reported), and audits
  `maintenance.enable` / `disable` on a flip, else `maintenance.update` (field names, the
  allowlist's size, the bypass, the day, `kvSynced`, the operator's IP).
  `GET /api/admin/settings/maintenance/preview?lang=` renders the saved page with status 200,
  private, noindex. A `site_profile` save refreshes the snapshot's buttons (`afterWrite`):
  only while both KV and the settings row say on, and the value it puts is the ROW's with the
  new buttons, never KV's own copy (a KV read can be a minute stale in its location; review
  finding u9-security-1).
- The reconcile (`src/lib/cron/maintenanceReconcile.ts`), a job of G2's 10-minute schedule:
  a "Back on" that came is written to the row (version-checked; a race is skipped and logged)
  and audited `maintenance.auto_disable`; the edge is healed toward off only, never switched
  on (a row on with the edge off is logged as an error for Site health); while both say on,
  the edge is set to the row's whole value (the bypass, the allowlist, the words, "Back on")
  with the buttons following the identity, compared field by field, so a stale value put
  back anywhere is healed within ten minutes (`synced`, logged). Budget 8; the schedule
  stays within 50.
- The panel (`/admin/maintenance`, a tab of General): the status banner, Turn on (a danger
  confirmation on the kit Modal) and Turn off, the page fields, "Who still sees the site",
  "What visitors see" (a class-based sketch) and "Preview full screen" in both languages; the
  top bar badge, the bell ("turned off by itself" when "Back on" ended it) and the dashboard
  follow the effective state.

## Departures from the design, and why

Recorded in `deviations.md` (U9's bullets and the §6.1 rows):

- `site_settings.timezone`, not B7.5's `site_profile.timezone` (the binding correction; C9a's
  0091 header and as-built still say `site_profile`, written before this PR).
- The zone is editable from a closed list of ten (`crm_settings`' list; the prototype offered
  five), in its own immediate card.
- Maintenance stays a tab, `/admin/maintenance` (D2), not a card inside General.
- The reconcile joins G2's 10-minute schedule instead of a new hourly trigger: one cron trigger
  fewer (the Free plan allows five) and the row follows the edge sooner.
- Page visibility keeps Riyadh days: U14a stores its windows' midnights at +03:00 and the
  edge compares instants, so following the zone means re-deriving saved windows; its guide
  says Riyadh, and General's guide says so too. Every other admin clock follows the setting.
- Copy made true (P-12): the WhatsApp hint without "the floating button", the Social sub-line
  without "the footer" until E6, no "Reset demo data", "The hero image" for "The hero video"
  (no video bytes on a 503), the built-in message in three forms matching the buttons shown.
- Socials at most 8 (the 0019 CHECK; the prototype 10). Logos read-only (build files, O-14).
- The job-applications switch is its own card on R3b's intake endpoint (immediate, never
  staged), not inside Advanced where Save would suggest it waits.
- The icon trigger names two constraints, one per column (the brief: one), so the 422 lands on
  the right field.
- No ribbon on bypassed public pages (D13). `site_settings.identity` left the form and the
  schema (D17); the column stays for a contract slice.
- With releases on, an identity save is staged and `afterWrite` does not run: R7's post-apply
  effects must refresh the maintenance buttons (the reconcile does within 10 minutes while
  maintenance is on). A staged icon that is not a square upload is refused at publish (the
  trigger under `apply_release`), not at save.
