## Plan (written before building; kept current)

Status: built (2026-11-10). The plan below is as it was written; "As built" after it records
what changed on the way and why.

### Who sees what

`crm.contacts` is Admin's alone (Developer none, owner default O-7; Sales from C10b). Every page
computes `can()` on the server and refuses any other role with a 403 and `Refused.astro`,
having read nothing (the U2 way, as the leads pages do); every route C6 built re-checks its
capability, RLS and the live check refuse again in the database. The flags handed to the islands
(`canErase`, `canAddLead` = `leads.manage` + `leads.pii`) are UX only.

### Screens

1. **People list, `/admin/contacts`** (`index.astro`, the `AdminContactsList` island,
   `screens/contacts/ContactsList.tsx`): head "Contacts", sub "{N} people · {n} clients · {n}
   leads · {n} past clients" from `POST …/summary`; "Add contact" (hidden until the island is
   up). The search (a form, posted in a body, never in the address; a whole e-mail or phone
   finds its person through the blind index). Chips with counts: All, Lead, Client, Past client,
   Partner, then Starred and Expiring soon. Selects with visible labels: Owner (Anyone, Me, No
   owner, a person), Sort (Last activity, Name, Starred first, Oldest), and a Tag box. The kit's
   DataTable at 20 a page: ☆ (one unversioned PATCH, the leads' late-answer rules); Person (name,
   title; "Phone match to check" in words when one waits); Company; Stage badge (Lead klein,
   Client ok, Past client gray, Partner sky); Tags (3 then "+n"); Owner; Last activity; Value
   (SAR, right-aligned). No contact detail, no consent column. Bulk bar: Add tag, Remove tag,
   Change owner…, Change stage…, Star (versioned except the star; conflicts said inline). Filters
   in the address through `src/lib/crm/contactUrl.ts` (an allow-list; never the search or the
   tag, which staff type and which can name a person).
2. **A contact, `/admin/contacts/[id]`** (`[id].astro`, the `AdminContactDetail` island,
   `screens/contacts/ContactDetail.tsx` and its cards): the tab says "Contact", the h1 is the
   name (`dir="auto"`), the sub-line "title at Company". Head controls: ☆, Stage and Owner
   selects (one PATCH each, versioned), tags (chips with Add tag). Left: the timeline (contact
   events and its leads' events, in sentences; chips All | Leads | Notes | Changes; Load older)
   and the notes (read on open through the audited door; "Only your team sees notes."; Add note;
   Delete for `crm.erase` after a confirm). Right: Contact details (masked e-mails and phones,
   "Reveal contact details (this access is logged)", then Email, Call and WhatsApp links, Make
   primary, Remove and Add an e-mail or phone, each one versioned PATCH, editable only after a
   reveal); Details (explicit Save: name, title, company, city, country, language, source, custom
   fields when C9b defines any); Leads (linked, newest first, a phone match asks "Is this the same

   Score ("Rules live in CRM settings"); Data ("Added {date} · last activity {ago} · kept until
   {date} unless there is new activity"). More actions → "Erase everything about them…"
   (`crm.erase`). Revealed values live in the island's state only.
3. **Add contact drawer** (lazy): Name\*, Title, E-mail, Phone or WhatsApp, Company, Stage
   (Lead), Owner (me), Language (English), City (no default), Source (Added by hand), Tags.
   "Give them a name"; a taken e-mail (409) says "Someone with this e-mail already exists" with
   an Open link. No consent toggles.
4. **New lead drawer** (lazy, from a contact): message\*, how they reached us, service, budget,

5. **Erase dialog** (lazy, Admin): the preview's counts and the phone matches as checkboxes,
   the reason (Their request | Our decision), the danger button "Erase everything about them",
   then a second confirm; "Logged in the audit log." and "Answer within 30 days."
6. **⌘K**: for `crm.contacts` holders the palette also posts the query to `POST
   …/contacts/lookup` (at most 8; name and company only) and lists a "Contacts" group. A query

   its GET search and its "See all results" link for it. (Superseded by the review: the record

### Departures from the design, and why

1. **No Source or City filter and no Value or Newest sort on the People list.** C6's filter
   (`app.crm_contact_filter`) takes stage, owner, starred, expiring, tag, the search, and the sorts
   recent, name, starred, oldest; adding keys is SQL in C6's held migration, and the screen offers
   what the API reads. Starred is a chip, beside Expiring soon.
2. **The tag filter stays out of the address**, as the search does (crm.md §11 names only `q`): a
   tag is words staff type, which can name a person ("URLs carry ids, never names").
3. **The notes are their own card above the timeline**, not a composer inside it: the timeline is
   metadata (C6 keeps a note's words behind the audited door), and the notes card is that door's
   reader, as on a lead's page. No Tasks chip (C8), Messages or Campaigns (LATER).
4. **No Add task** (C8) and **no Companies tab or Merge duplicates** (C11, C12). *(Add a
   task came with C8b at the C7 + C8 merge: C8's Tasks card on the contact's page, and the
   erase dialog's task count; see [C8](C8.md).)*
5. **New lead has no Service select**: the contact page does not read the services list (two more
   reads on every contact page); the interest can be named in the message.
6. **The erase dialog has no contact picker**: it opens on the contact's own page; the picker
   belongs with the CRM settings' "Erase a person's data" card (C9b).
7. **"Showing the newest 50 leads"** when a contact has more: the leads list has no contact filter
   (no SQL here), so the rest cannot be linked to yet.
8. **Not `useEntitySave`**: the Details card is a handwritten form with an explicit Save through
   the page's queue (as C4b's lead page, decision D3), so `entitySaveFields.spec` does not apply.
9. **The palette's address rule applies to every role**: the record search is a POST for
   everyone (review, C7-SEC-1), and "See all results" is left out for a term with an `@` or seven
   digits for anyone (no page or record is found by an e-mail address, and the address stays out
   of history and logs).
10. **No help guide here** (no migration in this slice): a contacts guide joins the help set with
    a later restatement.
11. *(Withdrawn by the review, C7-UX-1: it said the list's names had no `dir="auto"`, but the
    kit's DataTable already draws the row link with `dir="auto"`. Every place a contact's words
    are drawn now reads them in their own direction, the palette and the page head's sub-line
    included.)*
12. **No avatar in the contact page's head** (crm.md §11 screen 6 lists "avatar, name and ☆"):
    the h1 is the name, and the ☆ and the selects sit under it. An avatar of initials adds nothing
    a sighted reader does not already have in the h1; it can join with the Companies tab (C11).
13. **The New lead drawer has no Language field** (the plan's item 4 listed one): the route
    takes the language from the contact, which Details already edits, so one person has one
    language in both places.
14. **The add drawer's Company is free text** (crm.md §11 screen 7: "typeahead, or free text"):
    there are no company records to look up until C11 (Companies); the typeahead joins then.
