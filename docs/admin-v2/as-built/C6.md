### SQL (0133)

Reads are SECURITY INVOKER (RLS, the live check and the column grants decide; the Worker
names the tenant and gets nothing unless it is the token's, as `leads_list` does):

- `app.crm_contact_filter(jsonb)`: one allow-listed validator (stage, owner `me`/`none`/id,
  starred, expiring, tag, q, contact_kind + contact_hmac, sort, limit 1 to 100, offset to
  10 000); 22023 on anything else. A `q` that is an e-mail or phone is refused (found by its
  blind index only), as `app.lead_filter` does.
- `app.crm_contacts_with_channel(kind, hmac)`: a narrow definer, the ids of the caller's
  tenant's contacts holding that blind index, empty unless the caller's LIVE role holds
  `crm.contacts` (staff never read `value_hmac`).
- `app.crm_retention_months()`: the caller's own tenant's horizon (a definer wrapper over
  `app.lead_retention_months`, which staff cannot execute), for `expires_at`.
- `public.crm_contacts_list(p_tenant, p_filter)` → `{total, rows}`: safe columns, channel
  presence, linked lead count, value (won leads' `value_sar`, linked other than by phone) and
  score (the highest linked lead score, same rule), `expires_at`. Tombstones (`merged_into`)
  never listed.
- `public.crm_contact_summary(p_tenant)`: counts per stage, starred, mine, expiring soon
  (30 days), total.
- `public.crm_contact_timeline(p_tenant, p_contact, p_filter)`: the contact's events and the
  events of its leads linked other than by phone, one keyset `(at, src, id)`, at most 60, a
  kind filter (all, notes, leads, changes). Metadata only: note bodies stay behind the audited
  notes door.
- `public.crm_contacts_bulk_update(p_tenant, p_action, p_value, p_items)`: INVOKER, modelled
  on `leads_bulk_update`: tagAdd, tagRemove, owner, stage (versioned), star; applied,
  conflict, missing, skipped per item.

Writes that carry ciphertext, or touch tables staff cannot write, are service-role doors
(INVOKER, executable by the service role only) that check the acting profile live in SQL
(`app.crm_contacts_actor_ok`: active, unlocked, of the tenant, holding `crm.contacts`, and
`leads.pii` + `leads.manage` for a new lead):

- `public.crm_create_contact(p_tenant, p_contact, p_actor)`: the contact and its channels
  (the Worker encrypts and indexes); a taken e-mail answers `{duplicate: id}` under the link's
  own per-address advisory lock (no row written).
- `public.crm_edit_contact_channels(p_tenant, p_contact, p_version, p_add, p_remove,
  p_primary, p_actor)`: add (taken e-mail → duplicate), remove (writes `channel_removed`),
  make primary; the version the screen read is checked (40001), not moved.
- `public.crm_delete_contact_note(p_tenant, p_contact, p_note, p_actor)`: `crm.erase`
  (`app.crm_eraser_ok`), writes `note_deleted`.
- `public.crm_contact_link_lead(p_tenant, p_contact, p_lead, p_action, p_actor)`: the check C5
  left to C6. `confirm` turns a phone link into a `staff` link (re-linked, so C5's trigger
  writes the lines and runs the won and owner rules; the lead's e-mail and phone join the
  contact unless another contact holds the e-mail; its activity counts from then);
  `detach` ("not this person") leaves the lead unlinked for good (`contact_link 'staff'`,
  no contact) and removes the two link lines that named the stranger.
- `public.crm_contact_new_lead(p_tenant, p_contact, p_lead, p_actor)`: a lead from the
  contact's own ciphertext (primary e-mail and phone, copied, never decrypted) through
  `public.crm_ingest_lead`, then linked to this contact (`staff`) whatever the arrival link
  found.

### Departures from the plan or the design

- **Detail and edit in one route, channels apart.** PATCH changes the details (as the caller,
  through C5's column grant) or the channels (the service role's door), never both in one
  request (a 422 asks for two saves), so a refusal never leaves half a save.
- **Timeline audit:** `crm.contact.view` with `{page: first | more, kind}` (crm.md §9 says
  `{page}`), queued: it reveals nothing gated.
- **No open tasks in the detail** (crm.md §9): tasks are C8's.
- **A new lead's score has no company e-mail point.** The contact's e-mail is copied as
  ciphertext and never decrypted, so `company_email` cannot be worked out; the score can only
  be lower than an inquiry's, never higher.
- **The erasure preview indexes first, the erasure does not.** One batch (20 leads) of the
  tenant's own unindexed leads before `crm_erase_preview`; the erasure relies on the preview
  the Admin just read and reports `unindexed` again in its counts.
- **Custom values:** the definitions arrive with C9b, so until then any custom value is a
  422 ("There is no custom field called ..."), and `customFields` is `[]` in the detail.
- **Bulk action names** (C6 review, C6-COR-3): `tagAdd` and `tagRemove`, not crm.md §9's
  `addTag` and `removeTag`, following `leads_bulk_update` (0041) and its schema, so both bulk
  screens send the same words.
- **A timeline without notes, lead notes or tasks** (C6 review, C6-COR-3): crm.md §9 has the
  timeline UNION contact events, contact notes, linked lead events, lead notes and tasks.
  `crm_contact_timeline` unions the contact's events and its leads' events only (each note
  shows as its `note_added` line, never its words): a note's words stay behind the audited
  notes doors (`/notes`, fail-closed audit and a read limit), and tasks wait for C8.
- **The detail's leads and the notes say when they are cut off** (C6 review, C6-COR-7): the
  newest 50 leads (`moreLeads`) and the newest 200 notes (`more`), each in a total order
  (newest first, ties broken by id). No cursor yet: C7's screen links to the leads list
  filtered to the contact for the rest; older notes wait for a cursor if a person ever has
  more than 200.
- **A ceiling on a contact's channels** (C6 review, C6-SEC-3): a contact keeps at most ten
  e-mails and ten phones that a person adds or copies (`app.crm_channel_cap()`). A channel
  edit past it is refused whole (422, "A contact keeps up to 10 e-mail addresses and 10 phone
  numbers"); a confirmed phone match into a full contact still links but copies nothing of
  that kind and answers `full`. The arrival's own link (C5) is unchanged.
