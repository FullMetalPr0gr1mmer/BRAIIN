## The plan (written before the build, kept)

**U5, Services.** `/admin/services` the disciplines' overview, `/admin/disciplines/[id]` a
discipline's page with its services and the leads by service, `/admin/services/[id]` the editor
in tabs (Content, Case study, SEO, Leads, Publishing), Add service, Duplicate, the 4 and 5 caps.

the guide. Tests: pgTAP for the function, unit specs for every rule, e2e for the screens.

## What was built

### The data (0106)

- `public.lead_interest_counts(p_tenant uuid) returns jsonb`: `{ "<service slug>": n }` over the
  tenant's non-spam leads that name a service, the slug folded to lower case (the column is
  citext). SECURITY INVOKER, STABLE, `search_path ''`, empty for a tenant that is not the
  token's: leads' RLS, the RESTRICTIVE live check (`app.live_role()`) and the column grant
  (`tenant_id`, `service_of_interest`, `is_spam`, 0033/0034) decide what it counts, so the lead
  workers (Admin, Developer, Sales) count their own tenant and every other role, a stale token
  included, counts nothing. Counts only. EXECUTE revoked from PUBLIC, anon, authenticated and
  the service role, then granted to authenticated alone (P-14, both regimes). Postconditions:
  its shape, its callers (no PUBLIC ACL entry, no other grantee), the three columns readable
  through the grant and leads never table-wide.
- `services-and-projects`: a NEW guide through H11's catalog (`app.put_help_guide(…, 0)`, so the
  catalog holds it at revision 1), place **42** (free: 40 is saving-and-publishing, 45
  collections-and-faq, 62 and 85 U11's), 3 minutes, tag `content`, caps `services.write`,
  `portfolio.write`, `seo.entityMeta` (Admin, Content Creator, SEO read it), screens
  `/admin/services`, `/admin/disciplines`, `/admin/portfolio`; EN and AR block for block, filed
  as `docs/admin-v2/guides/services-and-projects.json` and put verbatim (the generator,
  scratchpad `u56_tools/gen_0106.py`, writes the literal from the file). `PAGE_GUIDES` moves the
  three screens from saving-and-publishing to it (that guide still names them, so the drawer
  lists both on those screens). Postconditions: the catalog row, its place alone, every tenant.

  em dash, P-12).

## Departures from the design, and why

1. **One PR, one migration** (the owner's folded-PR decision).
2. **The slug stays its writers'** (Admin, Content Creator), not "Admin and SEO, Content Creator
   read-only" (AV-14): that splits one row across two capabilities, which RLS cannot hold (CLAUDE.md
   §2, the Phase 3 amendment: such a split is enforceable in one layer only); the same as U8's
   posts. The field's help says a changed live address breaks the old one until a redirect points
   it there; "a 301 staged with the release" waits for releases (R12/R14). Owner question below.
3. **"Visible on the site" is the status** (Publishing tab, and the lists' eye), not a switch of
   its own: §8's one lifecycle, as U7's D7 for the collections.
4. **No `show_skip_pill`** (AV-14's optional migration): the pill shows, as today. It would be a
   column on a staged table (release fields, capture) for a switch nobody asked to flip; an
   expand-only follow-up when the owner wants it.
5. **Keywords stay bilingual lines**, not two chip lists: each English keyword is stored with
   its Arabic one, the shape the public chips read.
6. **No generated search column** (AV-15's optional migration): the list reads one page of 100 and
   searches it in the browser. A studio's catalogue is dozens of projects; above 100 the list
   says it shows the first 100 (the search then covers those).
7. **A discipline's card line and description stay optional**, as the schema holds them (AV-14
   drew them required); a card falls back without them.
8. **Duplicate and Archive** are in the editor (the status line's Duplicate, the status's
   "Archived" for an Admin), not head buttons: the head is server-rendered and the editor's own
   bar follows a save without a reload; "Open on site" and the way back are the editor's bar too.
9. **"Add a quote" opens the testimonial drawer with the project chosen** instead of creating an
   empty draft: a quote needs its words, author and consent record, written in the drawer where
   the consent CHECK applies; the save there creates the draft.
10. **The Case study tab is for the writers only**: SEO reads no `service_cases` (its read cap is
    `services.write`), so the tab is not offered rather than offered empty.
11. **The value cards' text is a paragraph** (`multiline`, five rows) as the prototype's textarea,
    both languages still required.
12. **SEO's "Used in" media links now include a discipline's page**: SEO opens it (the Services
    tab's own route) to reach a service's SEO; `mediaUsageLinks.spec` says so.
13. **`/admin/disciplines` is a redirect, with its API's refusal kept** (see Routes above).

## Rule 5: the deployed parsers and the push-to-deploy window

0106 adds a function and a guide; it changes no reader's shape. Between the push and the deploy
the old Worker never calls `public.lead_interest_counts` (nothing it ships names it), and the
help index shows `services-and-projects` to Admin, Content Creator and SEO (its caps are read
from the row), with no page-head "Guide" button on the three screens (the old `PAGE_GUIDES`
offers saving-and-publishing there). The guide describes the new screens, so for those minutes
it is ahead of the old ones; the order (push, then merge at once) keeps that short.

## Releases (P-3): still inert, and off means main

Services, disciplines, service cases and projects stay staged entities: every write goes through
the resource kernel (its staged branch behind `releasesEnabled()`), and nothing here adds a write
path or a staged table. With releases off every write is exactly main's: `releaseOffWrites`'
snapshot of every write is unchanged, and the only resource edits are list projections and
`fromRow` shaping. The fences hold as before: disciplines' published-parent fence, `service_cases`
links fenced to the writer's tenant (and its RESTRICTIVE published-parent fence), a project and
its children in one `save_portfolio()` transaction, the archive gates (H12).

## Public output

No public route, component, loader or schema the site reads changed: `packages/schemas/content.ts`
gains two constants and keeps its read schemas at 6. The render project (`tests/render`, the
public sections to the byte) passes unchanged; `served-html.e2e` runs in CI.

## Follow-ups and owner questions

- **The slug** (departure 2): keep it the writers' (today's rule, and U8's for posts), or make it
  SEO's (a §5 reading: "Per-entity SEO meta / slug / schema"), which needs its own table or a
  definer path to hold in both layers.
- **The read-back** counts the rows the tightened caps meet (`services_value_points_over_4`,
  `service_cases_problems_over_5`); expected 0 (the seed holds 3 of each). A row above them still
  renders and is refused at its next save until a card or a problem is removed.
- **`show_skip_pill`** (departure 4) when wanted.
- **The case study's linked project** list loads the whole projects list once for its preview;
  fine at a studio's size.
- **A quote's links are not fenced to the tenant** (found in review, before U6; not changed here):
  `testimonials.portfolio_id` and `client_id` are plain foreign keys (0021) and the testimonials
  resource has no `assertWritable` link check, unlike `service_cases` and `save_portfolio`. One
  tenant at launch, so nothing crosses today; "Add a quote" only starts the drawer with a project
  the person could pick there. A follow-up (the resource's `assertLinkInTenant`, and composite
  keys in an expand migration) closes it in both layers.
- **The Leads tab's 20**: the leads screen has no service filter yet (a C-lane follow-up could add
  `service` to `app.lead_filter`, so "All leads" could open filtered).
