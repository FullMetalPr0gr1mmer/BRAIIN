## The plan (written before the build)

### Scope

**U5, Services (AV-14).**
1. `/admin/services` becomes the Services overview (island `AdminServices`): the disciplines in
   a sortable list (poster thumb, "01 · Branding · <Arabic>", "N services · <card line>", the
   first four services as pills and "+N"), an eye per discipline that publishes or hides it
   (hiding a live one asks first: "Hides its N services too", the RESTRICTIVE
   `services_discipline_published`), the discipline drawer (name, card line, description,
   card clip, poster, status; Delete for Admin, the FK refusal said as "Move its services
   first"), Add discipline, the prototype's note, and the services filed under no discipline
   in a group of their own so none is unreachable. Archived rows follow H12/U7: only an Admin
   renumbers or writes one (`renumberable`); the eye is not offered on an archived row.
2. `/admin/disciplines/[id]` becomes the discipline's page (island `AdminDiscipline`): its
   services sortable inside the discipline's own numbers (a move reuses the numbers the
   discipline already holds, so no other discipline's order moves), an eye per service, Open
   on site, "N leads" per service and a "Leads by service" bar chart for `leads.manage`
   holders only (`GET /api/admin/leads/interest-counts`, counts with no personal data), the
   "Card on the site" preview, Edit discipline (the drawer) and Add service (a modal: English
   and Arabic name and the address, created as a draft in this discipline).
   `/admin/disciplines` answers 302 to `/admin/services`; the area's "Disciplines" tab goes
   (the overview is the disciplines); `/admin/disciplines/new` stays as the no-JS form.
3. `/admin/services/[id]` and `/new` (island `AdminServiceEditor`): tabs Content | Case study
   | SEO | Leads | Publishing on U8's EditorForm, with U9's groups inside Content (The
   service, Deliverables, The value we add, Hero, More). Value cards at most 4 ("Three cards
   work best"); the Case study tab is the `service_cases` row in its own form (its own
   version): linked project, the project's client and sector read-only, headline, where they
   were, problems (at most 5), results (at most 4), a preview card, and the design-sample
   banner with the way to make it real; Add a case study when the service has none. SEO is
   `SeoPanel`. Leads (leads.manage holders): the newest 20 non-spam leads naming the service,
   safe columns only (number, name, company, stage, received), read on the server. Duplicate
   (a draft copy, slug `-copy`, the case not copied) in the editor's bar; Archive stays the
   status (Admin only, the option hidden from other roles).
4. The admin write schemas take at most 4 value cards and 5 problems (the prototype's caps).
   The public read schemas and the database CHECKs keep 6, so no stored row stops rendering;
   the seed holds 3 of each, and the read-back counts production rows above the new caps.

**U6, Projects (AV-15).**
5. `/admin/portfolio` (island `AdminProjects`): "Projects", search over title and type in
   both languages, chips All / Featured / each sector / Hidden, a table with the poster thumb,
   title and type (Arabic below), service pills, sector, client, year, Featured and Hidden
   badges, Move up / Move down while the list is unfiltered (H12's `swapTarget`), Open Our
   Work, Manage tags (a wide drawer: Sectors edited in place for `categories.manage`, Clients
   linked to their drawer with the disclosure switch, the services as a link to Services) and
   Add project (a modal: English and Arabic title, the address and the type, a draft; never a
   clone).
6. `/admin/portfolio/[id]` and `/new` (island `AdminProjectEditor`): tabs Basics | Case study |
   Breakdown & gallery | SEO | Publishing. Basics holds the media card: poster, card clip and
   the hero banner (its video and its picture, two labelled controls). Case study holds the
   story, scope, results, the final film (video, poster, length shown) and the testimonial
   card (the linked quotes with their consent state, and Add a quote opening the testimonial
   drawer with this project preset). Breakdown & gallery holds the breakdown repeater and a
   keyboard-sortable square gallery grid with Remove buttons. Everything still saves through
   ONE `save_portfolio` call: the role-split controls are a view over the one `media` set
   (`projectMedia.ts`, pure), joined back in a fixed order on save, a stored item's unshown
   keys kept, the server's errors mapped back onto the control that holds the item. The
   publish-requirements panel mirrors `assertPublishable` (both titles, both types, a poster
   with alt text in both languages, no sample flag, no XX figures); the server stays the gate.

**Both.** Read-only for a role that may only view (SEO: the content read-only and its SEO tab
open; a Content Creator on an archived row the server would refuse), the SEO tab read-only at
`view`, "Open on site" from the saved values. A disabled fieldset now also stops a
pointer-dragged sort handle (U8's note), in the Sortable core. Services and projects stay
staged entities: every write goes through the resource kernel (and its staged branch, inert
while releases are off); no new write path, no public route touched.

### Migration (0106 placeholder)

1. `public.lead_interest_counts(p_tenant uuid) returns jsonb`: `{ "<service slug>": n }` over
   the tenant's non-spam leads that name a service. SECURITY INVOKER (leads' RLS, the
   RESTRICTIVE live check and the column grant decide), `search_path ''`, STABLE, empty for
   another tenant; EXECUTE revoked from PUBLIC, anon, authenticated and the service role, then
   granted to authenticated alone; postconditions.
2. `select app.put_help_guide($guide$ … $guide$::jsonb, 0);` the new `services-and-projects`
   guide at sort_order 42 (free: 40 is saving-and-publishing, 45 collections-and-faq; 62 and 85
   are U11's), caps `services.write`, `portfolio.write`, `seo.entityMeta`, screens
   `/admin/services`, `/admin/disciplines`, `/admin/portfolio`; filed as
   `docs/admin-v2/guides/services-and-projects.json`.
No table, column, policy or grant on a table changes, so the Sales boundary's catalog walk
needs no fixture.
