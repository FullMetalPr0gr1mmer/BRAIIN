## Plan (written before the build)

Releases stay off in production until R14. With releases off every save is live; with releases
on the singleton kernel stages the look and the section kernel stages the switch. There is no
second write path.

### Two parts, two capabilities (P-9, O-14)

- **On or off** (`pages.write`: Admin, Content Creator): the home page's first visible hero
  section's `content.intro`, written through the section kernel
  (`PATCH /api/admin/sections/[id]`, versioned, marked in release mode, R12b). Off stores
  `intro: false`; on removes the key, so the route's default (`withHomeIntro`) applies again.
  The page editor already keeps the key on its own saves (`SECTION_ADVANCED_ONLY`, `extra`).
  A home page with no visible hero shows no loading screen: the switch says so and is off.
- **The look** (`theme.edit`: Admin, Developer): five `intro_` columns appended to E5's
  `site_appearance` row, saved through E5's singleton (`PATCH /api/admin/site-appearance`),
  which sends only the keys a screen names, so the Header and Loading screens never write each
  other's columns.

### Storage: five `intro_` columns (`0124_loading_screen.sql`)

| Column | Values | Default (= the intro as built) |
|---|---|---|
| `intro_backdrop` | video, black, light | video (the logo over the hero loop) |
| `intro_bar` | boolean | false |
| `intro_tagline` | `{en, ar}`, 1 to 60 characters each (0098's plain-text shape), or null | null |
| `intro_hold_ms` | 500 to 1500, step 100 | 1000 |
| `intro_fade_ms` | 300 to 600, step 50 | 450 |

- One named CHECK per column (closed sets, the hold and fade steps), the Worker's Zod the other
  layer. Expand only: every row stored today takes the defaults.
- anon's column grant gains the five (the public intro reads them); authenticated and the
  service role keep their table grants. Postconditions restate anon's columns exactly, both
  grant regimes, MAINTAIN under the version CASE.
- The release registry's `site_appearance` row is restated (on conflict do update, every other
  field word for word) with the five columns appended.
- A new help guide, `loading-screen` (H11's put, revision 0, place 46, caps `pages.write` and
  `theme.edit`, screen `/admin/appearance/loading`).

### The public intro (Tier A, server-rendered)

- `getIntroSettings()` (`src/lib/data/appearance.ts`) reads the five columns through the
  content seam (the preview overlays a pending change), never throws; read on `/` and `/ar`
  only, in the routes' existing `Promise.all` (no round trip in series), and handed to the first
  hero as route `data` by `withHomeData` (never CMS content).
- `Hero.astro` emits on the plate a `data-*` attribute only for an option off its default
  (`data-backdrop`, `data-hold`, `data-fade`) and the tagline and the bar only when set, so with
  no row the plate is byte-identical.
- `global.css` maps each bucket to a fixed rule: `--bs-intro-hold` and `--bs-intro-fade`
  (inside `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`, so reduced motion still zeroes them); the
  choreography stays derived from those two tokens (hero-intro.md §1).
- **The LCP rule holds:** the logo's markup, box and entrance (from opacity .01) never change.
  The tagline and the bar hang below the logo, absolutely positioned (so the logo never moves),
  enter from opacity 0 (never an LCP candidate) with the logo's FOUC guard, and fade with the
  plate. The bar is a compositor-only `scaleX` over the hold, paused with the plate while the
  logo loads.
- **Opaque plates** (black, light) keep `pointer-events: none`, and lift the consent banner
  above them, so a consent click is never made on a banner nobody can see (PDPL).
- CSP unchanged (no inline style, no new origin, no new script).

### The admin screen: `/admin/appearance/loading`

- Appearance › Loading screen for `theme.edit` or `pages.write` holders (Admin, Content
  Creator, Developer); each card for its own capability; SEO and Sales get Refused and a 403.
- `AdminLoading` (screens/loading/): the switch card; the Style tiles (a native radio group,

  the fade (300 to 600 ms) through `useEntitySave` + `EditorForm` (Save, the 409, release mode,

## Departures

1. **The bar and the tagline are built** (ui.md "Not built: tagline and progress bar"). The
   README's E7 row ("all styles incl. bar and tagline; lhci re-gate") wins over ui.md, and the
   TDD row §6.1 loading says "built under A6". The LCP risk ui.md named is handled by
   construction (the logo never changes; the extras enter from opacity 0 below it) and by the
   re-gate.
2. **The styles are what sits behind the logo, and the bar and the tagline add to any of
   them.** The prototype's tiles mix grounds and marks (logo, black, bar, none) beside a
   Background select; here the tiles are video, black and light, and the bar and the tagline are
   their own controls. Every combination keeps the logo, which no style may drop (it is the
   home LCP element).
3. **No "None" tile.** The on/off switch is the home hero's `intro` (`pages.write`, O-14); a
   "None" style under `theme.edit` would be a second off switch, held by Developer, who does
   not hold the first. The switch is the one way to turn the intro off.
4. **No "Show only on the first visit" setting.** The intro is once per visit, in each tab
   (sessionStorage), always: the TDD's "once per session". The prototype's "remembered per
   browser" would need localStorage. It is a fact on the screen.
5. **Duration 500 to 1500 ms and fade 300 to 600 ms** (the prototype: 300 to 3000 and 100 to
   1500, with a toast past 1.5 s). ui.md's buckets: the site rule is never longer than 1.5 s,
   held by the schema and the CHECK, not a warning.
6. **The preview is a sketch played from the form, not the iframe with `?intro=replay`** (as E5
   and E6). With releases off (production until R14) the iframe shows only what is saved; the
   sketch follows the form before it is saved and costs no Worker render (P-15). "Open the
   site" is in the page head; "Play preview" is on the Preview card, not in the page head.
7. **An opaque plate lifts the consent banner above it** (not in the design). The plate keeps
   `pointer-events: none` (ui.md: "the consent click is never swallowed"), so over a black or
   light plate a click would reach a banner nobody can see; a consent choice must be seen
   (PDPL). Over the video the banner shows through the see-through plate, as built.
8. **The tagline is 1 to 60 characters in each language** (the prototype's field had no
   limit): 0098's plain-text CHECK, as the header button's words.
